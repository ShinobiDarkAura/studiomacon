What is missing

  • Emails have never been sent from Shopify. The templates are written, but Shopify's “send a test” has been unavailable, so none of the order emails have been seen in a real inbox.
  • No test order has been placed with a real card, so the full chain — pay, confirm, fulfil, track — is unproven.
  • Klaviyo's signing records at Cloudflare are set to “proxied”, which breaks its email signing and makes Klaviyo mail more likely to land in spam.
  • Shopify's own emails are still sent as a Shopify address, not an authenticated studiomacon.co sender.
  • The footer newsletter box needs a Shopify app token before it can add people to the customer list.
  • Klaviyo is being retired in favour of Shopify. Its welcome series is the only flow running; there are no cart or browse emails to lose.
  • Product photographs are missing inside Shopify, so checkout and receipts show grey boxes.

Alex1 · Un-proxy the email signing records

Cloudflare → studiomacon.co → DNS → Records. These are orange (“proxied”), which breaks signing. Open each, set Proxy status → DNS only, save:

dash.cloudflare.com → studiomacon.co → DNS → Records

kl._domainkey kl2._domainkey hello newsletter s1._domainkey s2._domainkey sel1._domainkey sg

Why: Klaviyo's welcome emails are signed with those records, and Klaviyo keeps sending until Shopify takes over. Unsigned mail from a domain that publishes DMARC is treated as suspicious.

Done 19 September — checked from outside Cloudflare; Klaviyo shows the domain Active.

Apple Pay — my recommendation

The Apple Pay button is now a plain Buy Now button: it builds the cart and goes straight to Shopify's checkout, where Apple Pay and the cards are offered. Apple's own guidelines forbid showing the Apple Pay mark on a button that does not open the Apple Pay sheet, so the mark is gone until it really does. The instant sheet only exists if the payment sheet runs on our own domain, which means either embedding Shopify's own accelerated-checkout button, or registering the domain with Apple and driving Shopify's Storefront API ourselves.

Recommendation: leave it as it is for launch. Embedding Shopify's button brings Shopify's styling into our layout, and the Storefront route is a week of work plus an Apple merchant verification that cannot even be started until the domain is live. Neither changes whether a sale completes. Revisit once ads are running and we can see how many people drop at checkout.
