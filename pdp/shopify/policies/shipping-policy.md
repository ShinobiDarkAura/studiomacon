# Shipping (draft — for Shopify Settings → Policies)

**Making your piece.** Each piece is made to order. Please allow up to 10 business days for it to be made before it ships.

**United States.** Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days after shipping. Rates are calculated at checkout.

**International.** Delivery generally takes 5–10 business days after shipping, depending on the destination. Rates are calculated at checkout. Duties and import taxes may be charged by your country on arrival.

**Tracking.** A tracking link is emailed as soon as your piece ships.

**Custom commissions.** Timelines are agreed with you when the commission begins.
