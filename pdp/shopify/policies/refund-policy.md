# Returns & Exchanges (draft — for Shopify Settings → Policies)

Every piece is made to order, by hand, so each one takes a little time to reach you.

**Returns.** Pieces in their original condition may be returned within 14 days of delivery for store credit toward a future piece. Custom commissions and one-of-a-kind pieces cannot be returned.

**Sizing & exchanges.** If a ring or cuff doesn't fit, write to us and we'll gladly help find the right size.

**Starting a return.** Email hello@studiomacon.co with your order number and the reason for your return. You'll receive a return shipping label and instructions. Once the piece is received and checked, store credit is issued and confirmed by email.

**Damaged or incorrect pieces.** If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, write within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we'll make it right.
