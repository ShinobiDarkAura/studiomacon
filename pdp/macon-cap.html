A tasteful cap adorned with a mystic symbol. It offers a modicum of protection from the sun, and a considerable amount of protection from people who might otherwise ask what you've been making lately. Wear it to the studio, or anywhere.

We made these because we each wanted one, with our mark on the front and a little shade for long days outside. They are made in small runs, so when a colour is gone, it may be gone for a while.

Each piece begins as a sketch on paper. It is carved by hand, first in wax, then fine-tuned in metal. A local foundry casts the wax carving in bronze or silver, and the piece comes back to our studio to be refined and polished.

Collection pieces are made from a mold of that first original, so every one is a child of the piece we carved.
