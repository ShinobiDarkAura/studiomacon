A Horn for Undiscovered Chords

Ida's Wonder Horn

$390

Description

A spiral resonator in sterling silver, worn long on a thirty inch chain. Ida played hers until the goblins danced out the window — a story Hannah has known since she was small, and the one this horn was made to remember.

Lab equipment is said to vibrate sympathetically in its presence. Nearby children have been known to develop perfect pitch.

Wearing & Care

Worn long, the horn rests just below the breastbone and turns with you as you move.

Sterling silver darkens with time and air. Wear it often and it stays bright; bring back the shine with a soft polishing cloth. Take it off to swim, and store it on its own so the spiral stays unscratched.

Materials

100% sterling silver, on a thirty inch chain.

The Casting

Each piece begins as a sketch on paper. It is carved by hand, first in wax, then fine-tuned in metal. A local foundry casts the wax carving in silver, and the piece comes back to our studio to be refined and polished.

Collection pieces are made from a mold of that first original, so every horn is a child of the one Hannah carved.
