Rings

Finding
Your Size

Two ways to find it. Printing the sizer is the most accurate; measuring a ring you already wear is the easiest. If the piece arrives and it isn't right, we resize or exchange it.

1. Check the print

Print this page at 100%, with page scaling and “fit to page” switched off. Then hold a ruler against the bar below. It must measure exactly 100 mm, or roughly 3 15⁄16 inches. If it doesn't, the print was scaled and the sizer will be wrong.

050 mm100 mm

2. Cut and wrap

Cut out the strip below and the small slot in it. Wrap the strip around the base of your finger, pointed end first, and feed it through the slot.

Pull to fit, then read the size

Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest, and warm. A ring should pass the knuckle with a little resistance.

Or measure a ring you own

Take a ring that fits the right finger and measure straight across the inside, from edge to edge, in millimetres. Match it below.

Still unsure? Write to hello@studiomacon.co with the finger and hand, and we will talk it through. Every ring is made to order, so it is worth getting right the first time.
