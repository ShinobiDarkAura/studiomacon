Custom Heirlooms

Let's Make Something Real Together

It's a rare privilege to create custom heirlooms that honor a special moment. If you're interested in commissioning a piece for yourself or someone you love, we would love to hear from you.

Begin a Commission

Every commission begins as a sketch on paper. Once the drawing feels right, the piece is carved by hand in wax, cast by a local foundry in bronze, silver or gold, and refined and polished in our studio. A custom piece is never molded or repeated. It is one of a kind.

Write to us about the person or the moment you'd like to honor, and anything you already imagine: a material, a shape, a story. We'll take it from there. Contact us or email hello@studiomacon.co.
