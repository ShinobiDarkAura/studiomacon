      Export for Shopify

        1. In Shopify, open Messaging → Templates and choose Create template, then paste the HTML and save.
        2. From Messaging, Create message → Email, pick that template, and paste the subject and preview text.
        3. Send yourself a test, look at it on your phone, then schedule or send.
