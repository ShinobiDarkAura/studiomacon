BRONZE
The patina darkens where the thumb rests and brightens where it rubs. To keep it as it is, wipe with a soft dry cloth. Avoid polish; it takes the patina with it.
SILVER
Silver darkens slowly in the air. A polishing cloth brings it back; wearing it often does the same.
