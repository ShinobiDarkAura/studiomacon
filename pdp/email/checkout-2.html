Is it solid?
Every piece is cast solid in bronze, silver or gold. Nothing is plated, and it has real weight in the hand.
When does it ship?
It is made by hand once it is chosen, and it leaves the studio in California within about ten business days.
Not quite right?
Rings are made to size and remade if the fit is off. Any piece can be returned for store credit in 14 days.
Anything else?
A reply to this email reaches the studio directly, not a help desk. Every note is read and answered in turn.
