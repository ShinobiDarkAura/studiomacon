Dressed reference plates — the shelves with the vintage props still on them.

instruction to remove the objects and change nothing else, which is why the light,
the grade and the camera match exactly. Any future plate should be emptied from a
dressed reference rather than described from scratch — writing one from a
description produced something cleaner and more architectural, and it was wrong.
