Eyebrow & Title

Every email opens the same way: the eye mark, then a small black eyebrow, then a serif headline. Bag and browse emails swap the headline for a larger "title" with a short rule under it — a size that sits between the site's own h1 sizes.

Anatomy

Eye mark (54px, padding 53px 0 44px) → eyebrow (14px/1.4 sans, black, 17px below) → either h1() (46px serif, 51px below by default) or title() (54px serif, 8px above, 24px below, then a 48×1 black rule with 40px below). h1 and title never appear together.

PartValueToken
Eye mark54px wide, padding 53px 0 44px—

Rule under title48×1px black, 40px below--rule-email-title

The site's own h1 runs 46 / 53 / 60 across breakpoints (--t-h1, --t-h1-md, --t-h1-lg). Email h1 stays a fixed 46px on desktop and drops straight to 40px on phones — it never touches 53 or 60. The title-with-rule variant, at 54px, sits between the site's mid (53) and large (60) h1 sizes but is its own thing: only email bag/browse copy uses it.

Phones (max-width:620px)

.h1 becomes 40px!important. .pad gets 24px side padding (same as desktop — email padding does not change on phones). Everything else in this component is unaffected.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Use h1() for welcome/thanks/order emails, title() for bag/browse emails — never mix them in one email.
  • Keep the eye mark first, every time, at 54px.
  • Let the two-line headline break with a literal <br>, not word-wrap.
Don't
  • Don't reuse the site's 53px or 60px h1 sizes in email — email h1 is fixed at 46, full stop.
  • Don't add the black rule under a plain h1 — it belongs to title() only.
  • Don't invent a phone size other than the documented 40px.
