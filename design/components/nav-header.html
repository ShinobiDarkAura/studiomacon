Nav Header

The fixed header that rides over every page. It never paints a background of its own — instead each part of it (the links, the centre logotype, the Bag link) reads the luminance of whatever is directly beneath it and flips between black and white on its own.

Anatomy

  • Left — "Menu" on phones (opens the drawer); "Shop / Custom / About" on desktop
  • Centre — the logotype, a CSS mask of logotype.png painted in currentColor, so it recolours with the rest of the nav
  • Right — "Bag (0)", with a toast that can drop beneath it and a bump animation when a piece is added
  • Back — an optional fourth part that rides into the nav row on desktop product/custom pages

The big-logotype hero (shop.html)

Artifacts of Love

static frame — scale 2.8125, centred on the film

On the shop page only, the nav's own logotype starts 2.8125× its normal size, centred on the film, with the tagline "Artifacts of Love" set in Henry italic (15pt) beneath it. As the visitor scrolls, the logotype is scrubbed — not eased on a timer — from that size down into its normal nav position over max(240px, min(45vh, 560px)) of scroll. If nothing is scrolled for 8 seconds it fades where it sits (body.hero-quiet) and hands off to the scroll cue; once it has travelled the whole distance it docks for good (body.logo-docked) and never flies back down, even at the very top of the page.

Responsive

Below 768px: Menu replaces Shop/Custom/About, the logotype grows slightly (108px) and sits 4px higher, the nav shifts 8px higher overall, Back disappears, and the header padding switches to the 28px phone inset rather than the 64px desktop edge.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Let the nav tone itself — never hardcode black or white on a new page
  • Keep the logotype a mask on currentColor, never a flat image
  • Leave Back off phone layouts entirely
Don't
  • Give the header a background colour or blur — it must never paint one
  • Move Bag's on-dark override to phones — there is no white column there
  • Replay the big logotype hero anywhere but the shop page's film
