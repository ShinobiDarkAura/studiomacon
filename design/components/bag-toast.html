Bag Toast

A small confirmation that appears under the Bag link the moment a piece is added — a thumbnail, the piece's name and size, and its price — then clears itself after 2.6 seconds.

Anatomy

The toast is appended once, inside .head .right, and reused for every add. It carries a 40×50 thumbnail, a bold "Added to bag" label, the piece name and size, and a muted price note. The Bag link itself bumps (a quick scale) each time.

Live

Press Add to Bag — the toast appears under the link, the counter and the link itself bump, and it clears after 2.6s.

Responsive

The toast is capped at calc(100vw - 2 * var(--gutter)) on phones so it can never run past the screen edge; on desktop it simply sizes to its content, right-aligned under the Bag link.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Reuse one toast element per page, refilling it on every add, rather than stacking a new one each time.
  • Bump the Bag link every time, even if the toast is still on screen from a previous add.
  • Leave out the size segment entirely when a piece has none, rather than showing an empty "· Size".
Don't
  • Don't let the toast block interaction — it stays pointer-events:none until it's on.
  • Don't extend the 2.6s dwell time; it's tuned to be noticed without lingering.
  • Don't reposition the toast away from the Bag link — it belongs directly under it, right-aligned.
