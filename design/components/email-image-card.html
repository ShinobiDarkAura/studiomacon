Phones

None of these three blocks have a phone-specific rule in build_emails.py — width:100% and max-width already do the shrinking, and .pad only changes the outer 24px side padding.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Use 16px radius and the lift shadow on every email photo — it is the one radius/shadow pair emails use everywhere.
  • Keep the Klaviyo scrim at .42, not the site's .45 — it is intentionally a touch lighter over a smaller image.
  • Show the peek strip only when a second piece exists in the bag.
Don't
  • Don't use the site's 6px photo radius in an email — email photos are always 16px.
  • Don't put a background-image card into a Shopify template; their checker rejects it — use the plain photo card instead.
  • Don't skip the bgcolor fallback; some clients never load the background-image at all.
