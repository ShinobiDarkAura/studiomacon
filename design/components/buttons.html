Buttons & Links

Every button and link treatment on the site: the full-width black button that carries an arrow out on hover, its shorter product-page cousin, Apple Pay, the phone bag dock, choice buttons, and the underlined text links used for navigation and secondary actions.

Anatomy

Every button on the site is square-cornered and fills the width of its column — there is no site button sized to its label. The primary black button (.cta/.next) always carries the same sliding arrow, built from one inline SVG mask reused at three sizes (14px, 12px, and the arrow is absent on outline/choice/qty controls).

Responsive

All buttons stay full width in their column at every size — none of them shrink to fit their label. The phone dock only appears on phones (site.css .dock, hidden ≥768 in template.html). Apple Pay switches to the native OS button under @supports (-webkit-appearance:-apple-pay-button) in Safari, hiding the custom markup entirely.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep buttons square-cornered and full-width in their column.
  • Write labels in Title Case in the markup — CSS uppercases them.
  • Show only one black button per screen region (the paired Add to Bag / Apple Pay is the one exception).
Don't
  • Round a button's corners or size it to its text.
  • Introduce a grey button — the site has only black, outline and white/bordered.
  • Resize the arrow mask independent of its button's own scale (14/12px are the only two sizes in use).
