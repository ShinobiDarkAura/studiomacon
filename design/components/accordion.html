Studio Maçon · Accordion

Accordion

A row that opens on a grid-row transition, so any length of copy expands at the same steady pace. Used for a piece's Care/Materials/Sizing/Shipping, the general shipping policies, and the Custom order's locked-in-order steps.

Anatomy

.acc-item holds a .acc-head button (a plus icon drawn from two gradients, rotating to a minus when open) and an .acc-panel > .acc-inner > .acc-body. The row's own bottom rule darkens to black on hover; the body fades and rises in with a small delay once it opens.

Responsive

No structural change on phones — the accordion sits inside .info/.acc, which switches to left-aligned text and the phone gutter, but the row, head and panel mechanics are identical at every width.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Let the grid-row transition handle height — never measure or animate a fixed pixel height in script.
  • Keep the plus icon at .35 opacity at rest; it only reaches full black on hover or while open.
  • Lock later Custom steps until the one before them is done, and mark a finished step "Done" rather than just closing it.
Don't
  • Don't open a locked step by any means other than finishing the step before it.
  • Don't skip the 0.08s body delay — it is what keeps the open feel deliberate rather than instant.
  • Don't restyle the accordion for the Custom steps beyond number, Done tick, and lock state — it's the same component.
