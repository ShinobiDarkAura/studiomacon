Provenance Stamp

The Bureau of Provenance mark, pressed onto the shop hero's headline once it has settled into view — a single, deliberate press rather than a decoration that is simply there.

Anatomy

  • Headline — "Small Things, Made to Last," the stamp lands on the comma
  • Stamp — the provenance-stamp.png artwork, rotated and scaled down as it presses

At rest the stamp is scaled 1.35× and invisible; pressing runs it down to scale 1 and fades it in over .45s, cubic-bezier(.3,1.4,.5,1) — the eased overshoot reads as a hand coming down and catching. On the live page this fires automatically, 500ms after the headline has scrolled 90% into view.

Why it is measured in em

Every measurement — width, left offset, top offset — is written in em against the headline's own font size, not the viewport. That is what lets the stamp land on the comma in "Small Things," at 390px and at 1440px alike: as the h1 resizes across breakpoints (46px → 53px → 60px), the stamp resizes and repositions with it automatically, with no separate media query of its own.

Responsive

No breakpoint changes the stamp's own rules; it tracks the h1's font-size at every width. The section it sits in (.welcome) allows horizontal overflow on phones because the stamp may bleed slightly off the right edge there.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the stamp pinned to a headline, never to the viewport
  • Trigger the press once, on first view, not on every scroll into frame
  • Use the exact provenance-stamp.png artwork
Don't
  • Resize the stamp in px — it must stay in em with the headline
  • Change the -14deg tilt
  • Reuse the press animation for anything other than this stamp
