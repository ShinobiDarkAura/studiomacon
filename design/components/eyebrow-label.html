Eyebrow & Label

The two overlines on the site. .eyebrow sits above every page's h1 and reads in sentence case. .label is the smaller, always-uppercase tag used for field names, section markers and counts.

Anatomy

.eyebrow — sans, 15px (16px ≥768), sentence case, margin:0 0 10px, always the line directly over an h1.
.label — sans, 13px, letter-spacing:.14em, uppercase, rgba(0,0,0,.5).

The .prose inconsistency

About Maçon

Because .prose p sets font-size:17px and outranks .eyebrow's 15/16px by specificity, an eyebrow placed inside a .prose block renders at 17px instead of its usual size — visible on the home welcome block and Story's "Our Practice".

Unstyled here as the site actually renders it: 17px, not 15/16px. pages/shop.html .welcome .prose; pages/story.html .prose (site.css line 144, .prose p{font-size:17px}).

Responsive

The only size change is .eyebrow itself: 15px on phones, 16px at 768px and up. .label never changes size across breakpoints. On phones every title (including its eyebrow) is centred (site.css line 249).

Do / Don't

Do
  • Write .eyebrow copy in sentence case — "Say Hello", not "SAY HELLO".
  • Use .label for anything that names a field, section or count, in uppercase.
  • Keep exactly one .eyebrow directly above one h1 per section.
Don't
  • Put an eyebrow inside a .prose block without checking it doesn't quietly become 17px.
  • Reuse the phone-only Field Notes variant (black, .08em) anywhere else.
  • Stack two .label rows without at least a field's worth of space between them.
