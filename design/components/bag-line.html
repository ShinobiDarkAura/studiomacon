Bag Line

One row per piece in the bag: thumbnail, name and quantity stepper on the left, running total on the right. The order summary sits beside it; an empty bag and the checkout mini-row use lighter versions of the same parts.

Anatomy

A .line is a three-column grid — image, details, amount. The thumbnail carries --shadow-lift, the only shadow ever placed on a site photograph (the same shadow used on every email photo and card). The quantity stepper is two round buttons around a number, with Remove set apart to its right.

Responsive

≥900px the bag becomes a 1fr / 340px grid, gap 64px, with the summary sticky at top:calc(var(--head) + 24px). Below that the summary sits directly under the lines, full width, not sticky.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the lift shadow only on bag/email photographs — it never appears on a shop-wall or gallery picture.
  • Show the outline Checkout button only when the bag is empty; solid black otherwise.
  • Carry a piece's size into the meta line ("$260 · Size 7") whenever it has one.
Don't
  • Don't let quantity drop below 1 — the row removes itself instead.
  • Don't add a shadow anywhere else on the page to "match" the bag; it's deliberately singular.
  • Don't call Remove a button style — it's an underlined text link, not a black or outline button.
