Phones (max-width:620px)

trio() (pieces): the third tile (class "duo gone") disappears and the remaining two widen to 50% each, so the row always shows two pieces on a phone. film_steps() (how it's made): no such rule — it stays three-up, only shrinking type via .trio-t/.trio-d. steps(): its cells carry class="col", so on a phone they stack to full width, one drawing per row, instead of staying three-across.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Always pair friends() with a trio() of pieces — it never appears alone.
  • Keep film_steps() at three stills; it is meant to read as a small filmstrip.
  • Use the .duo / .duo gone classes on trio() cells so phones drop to two, never three squeezed tiles.
Don't
  • Don't add a shadow to trio() piece tiles — only film_steps() stills carry the lift shadow.
  • Don't change trio() to show three pieces on phones; the layout was built for two.
  • Don't reuse steps()'s 40px side padding elsewhere — most rows use 24px.
