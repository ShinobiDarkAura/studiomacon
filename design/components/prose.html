Studio Maçon · Prose

Prose

The site's body copy: the default sans paragraph, centred ledes, product descriptions, accordion bodies, and the smaller notes and fine print scattered around checkout and forms.

Anatomy

Body default is 17px/1.5. .prose p and .lede both run 17px/1.55, centred, max-width 620px. On phones every title in a prose block is centred too. Product description (.desc) is smaller — 15px on phones, 16px desktop — because it shares column space with the price and buttons rather than standing alone.

Voice

Voice
  • Site copy is third-person field notes — "Maçon makes…", "Each piece is…" — never "we" or "I" anywhere on the site.
  • Customer emails are the one exception: they speak as "we" ("We'll write again the moment it's cast…").
  • Pieces are artifacts — never "products," and never sales or stock language ("in stock," "buy now," "limited").
  • Never "talisman" or "heirloom."
  • No exclamation marks anywhere on the site.

Responsive

.desc steps from 15px to 16px at 768px. On phones every prose block, and every title inside one, is centre-aligned; above 768px, .prose stays centred but sits in its own column rather than filling the page width.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Write in third person field notes, active voice, plain description.
  • Keep prose paragraphs inside the 620px column and centred.
  • Use "we" only inside a customer email, never on the site itself.
Don't
  • Use "talisman," "heirloom," or sales language like "in stock" or "buy now."
  • Add an exclamation mark anywhere.
  • Call a piece a "product" — it's an artifact or a piece.
