Flag for normalising: the email button is heavier (500 vs 400), smaller (12 vs 15.333px), more tightly tracked in absolute terms but reads looser because the type is smaller, and shaped to its label rather than the site's fixed 55px-tall full-width bar. It works fine on its own inside an email, but it is not the same component as the site button — treat any effort to unify them as a real design decision, not a bug fix.

Phones

No phone-specific override for the button in build_emails.py; it keeps its padding and type size at every width.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Use "Browse artifacts" for shop-facing emails, "Return to checkout" for abandoned-checkout emails.
  • Give "Return to checkout" the extra 12px of air above it (16px vs. 4px).
  • Keep the button its own table cell — mail clients need the table wrapper, not just an <a>.
Don't
  • Don't assume the email button and the site .cta are interchangeable — they differ in weight, size, tracking and width.
  • Don't stretch the email button full-width; it is meant to size to its label.
  • Don't drop the bgcolor attribute — some mail clients need it alongside the inline background.
