Footer

The footer that closes every page: the loop mark and fine line, the site links, and a newsletter signup. It reorders itself between phones and desktop rather than only resizing.

Anatomy

  • Brand — the loop mark and "Artifacts of Love, Made to Order."
  • Middle — the site links: About, Custom, Shipping & Returns, Inspiration, Contact
  • News — one line of copy and an email field with Submit

Responsive

Below 768px: brand, links and newsletter stack and centre, in the order news → links → brand, min-height 75vh once the phone gutter media query stacks it (85vh at the unstacked breakpoint boundary). At 768px and up: three columns (1fr auto 1fr), brand left, links centred, newsletter right, min-height 20vh.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the fine line exactly "Artifacts of Love, Made to Order."
  • Use body.fit on any page short enough that the footer would otherwise float far below the fold
  • Keep the newsletter field a single hairline rule, no box
Don't
  • Reorder the desktop columns — brand/middle/news is fixed
  • Add a border or background behind the footer
  • Change the newsletter copy per page — it is one line everywhere
