Product Gallery

The product page's whole composition: photographs running full-bleed down the left, an information column pinned beside them. On a phone the two stack — one photo, a thumbnail strip, then the details, centred.

Anatomy — desktop (≥768px)

A 70fr/30fr grid, gap 48px, flush to the page's right margin. The gallery sits 12px in from the left edge; each photograph runs one full screen tall, 12px apart, and every picture after the hero runs 20% taller. The info column is position:sticky, pinned to top:var(--head), and centres its own contents vertically with justify-content:safe center so nothing is ever cut off if the column is short.

Field Notes

A small bronze figure, cast from a single carving, meant to sit in a pocket or on a shelf.

Scaled down to fit the page — each figure is a fraction of its true one-screen-tall height. Widths and the 12px rhythm are exact.

Anatomy — phones

The hero runs 4:5, radius 6px, 8px in from the screen edge. A thumbnail strip (radius 4px, gap 8px) follows, one entry per picture or film, sharing the width evenly. The info column then centres everything below it.

Responsive

Below 768px the split collapses: the gallery shows only the hero (other figures are hidden, replaced by the thumbnail strip), and the info column is no longer sticky — it flows centred beneath the hero. The .dock bag bar exists only on phones; ≥768px it is display:none in favour of the in-column Add to Bag row.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the info column's own scroll independent of the gallery — it is a sticky, internally-scrolling panel, not a fixed sidebar.
  • Let each photograph run the full height of a screen; never crop a gallery image to a fixed pixel box.
  • Show the dock only after the real Add to Bag button has scrolled away.
Don't
  • Don't add a shadow or a background colour to a gallery figure — it uses the plain --field placeholder until the photo loads.
  • Don't stretch phone thumbnails past 4:5 — however many exist, they share the width evenly.
  • Don't remove the "Back" link's fade-out; it belongs to the product column and disappears once that column has scrolled past.
