Footer & Body Text

The footer closes every email the same way: a thin rule, a line about the studio, the About / Instagram / Unsubscribe links, and the organisation's own name and address. Above it, the body text() block sets the column width copy sits in — three widths depending on the email.

Voice

Email voice vs. site voice
  • Customer emails speak as "we": "We're so glad you're here", "We saved it for you", "We start on your order as soon as it comes in."
  • The site itself never uses we/I — it stays in third person, the same as the rest of the copy voice on studiomacon.co.
  • Never write "talisman" or "heirloom" — in either voice.
  • Pieces are "pieces" or "artifacts" ("Browse artifacts"), never "products".

Phones

.pad keeps the footer and text() blocks at 24px side padding on phones (the same as desktop). The 378/338/294px max-widths are already narrower than a typical phone viewport, so they do not need a separate phone rule.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the footer identical across every email — rule, line, links, organisation.
  • Pick the text() column width (378 / 338 / 294) to match how much is being said — narrower for shorter, more pointed copy.
  • Write customer emails in "we", matching "We saved it for you" and "We're so glad you're here."
Don't
  • Don't give the footer links their own colour — they stay the same grey as the surrounding fine print.
  • Don't invent a fourth text() width; only 378, 338 and 294 are in use.
  • Don't slip into "we" on the site itself, or into third person inside a customer email — the two stay separate.
