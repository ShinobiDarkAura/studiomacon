Scroll Cue

A small mouse outline with a drifting dot, shown over the shop page's film once the visitor has sat still on it for a while — a quiet nudge to keep scrolling rather than an instruction.

Anatomy

  • Outline — a rounded 22×34 pill, faint white border
  • Dot — 4px white, loops down and fades inside the outline

Live

Shown on a dark frame, standing in for the film. The cue itself never fades in on its own timer in this demo — toggle it to see the state after 8 seconds of stillness (body.hero-quiet) on the real page.

Responsive

No change in size or position between phones and desktop; it only ever appears on the shop page's full-screen film, which fills the viewport at every width.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep it on dark or photographic grounds only — it is a white outline
  • Let it wait the full 8 seconds before appearing
  • Hide it the instant scrolling starts
Don't
  • Show it on any page besides the shop hero
  • Speed up or shorten the loop — 2.8s is deliberately slow
  • Run it under reduced motion — the dot's animation is switched off
