Principles
Observed

Not aspirations: these are the rules the site already keeps, everywhere. Break one and the page stops looking like Maçon.

Black on white, nothing else

Two colours. Every grey is black at an opacity, never a mixed hex. The only accent is the red of a missed size (#c8321e), and the only yellow is the code highlighter in email.

The nav has no background, ever

Menu, logotype and Bag float over whatever is beneath and read it: black over anything paler than 82% luminance, white over anything darker. The whole row switches as one.

Serif titles, always uppercase

Instrument Serif is only ever set in capitals, weight 400, line-height 1, tracked −.005em. The two exceptions are italic: the Story step names and the film tagline in Henry.

Helvetica Neue does the talking

Body is 17px/1.5. Everything small is Helvetica: overlines at 13px tracked .14em, buttons at 15.33px tracked .08em, prices, meta, forms. Weight 500 is reserved for a picked size, a total, and the toast label.

Photographs sit in 6px frames

Every framed picture on the site (tiles, related cards, the phone hero, bag thumbnails, masonry) has a 6px radius on a #f7f7f7 field. Full-bleed photographs have none. Emails use 16px and a soft drop shadow.

Buttons are square and full width

The black button is 55px tall, spans its column, has no radius, and grows an arrow to the right of the words on hover. On the product page it is 47px and 15% smaller. There is no grey button.

One gutter on phones

Text, buttons and grids sit 16px from the screen edge on every page; Menu and Bag a further 12px in. Photographs that run edge to edge are left alone; the shop grid and hero sit 8px in.

Rules are hairlines

Dividers are 1px #e3e3e3 and darken to black on hover. Form fields are underlines only, .5px, never boxes. The one bordered box is the size picker.

Things drift in, they do not pop

Blocks arrive from 66px below through a 16px blur over 1.1s on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), staggered. Hover is slower than you think: underlines take .45s, tile zooms 1.2s. Reduced motion turns it all off.

Generous, centred space

Sections are 96px apart on phones and 140px on desktop; the page starts head + 36px down. Titles are centred on phones. Prose is never wider than 620px.

Third-person field notes

The site describes; it never says "we" or "I", never exclaims, never sells. Pieces are artifacts. Emails, written to a person, may say "we".

Pieces are shown in the hand

The tile's hover image, the email hero, the bag thumbnail: the piece held, worn or resting at home. Scale reads through a hand, not a ruler.

Colour

Black, white, and black at an opacity. Contrast is shown against white and against black (WCAG ratio; AA for text is 4.5, for large text 3). Greys under 50% opacity are decorative, not for reading.

Ink and paper

Ink at an opacity (over white)

Fields and placeholders

Email only (flat hex for mail apps)

The site's greys cluster into three roles: rules (.2–.25), faint (.35–.45: placeholders, counts, Back at rest) and muted text (.5–.55: overlines, prices, notes). The values .22, .28, .3, .62 and .65 each appear once; see Inconsistencies.

Spacing

Radii, Shadows
& Rules

Photographs are the only things with corners. Buttons, fields, the size box and the footer are square. Shadows exist in exactly two places on the site and everywhere in email.

Voice

The site is written as field notes in the third person. Emails are written to a person and may say "we". Two words are banned outright.

Site · third person, present tense"Maçon makes artifacts and jewelry to be carried, worn and handed down."
"Each piece is individually made to order, so it takes a little time to reach you."
"Bronze is meant to be handled. The patina darkens where your thumb sits."
Email · "we", warm, still plain"We're so glad you're here!"
"We saved it for you."
"A second order means a lot to a studio our size."
Never"talisman" · "heirloom" · "product" · "shop now" · "limited stock" · "buy" as a verb in a title · exclamation marks on the site · "we" or "I" on the site (the Story and Contact pages are the current exceptions, see Inconsistencies).
Words that are Maçonpiece · artifact · totem · carried, worn, handed down · made to order · cast solid · patina · in the hand · the studio in California · Kindred · Field Notes · Also Worth Keeping.
